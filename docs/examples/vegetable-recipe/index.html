Pick two. Get a recipe.

Choose any two of 27 vegetables and download a one-page A4 recipe card (PDF) for 2 servings, with amounts in g, times in min and oven temperatures in °C. Nutrition is per 100 g of raw vegetable (USDA, provisional) and prices are in € per kg (MAPA, July 2026). The method is a general suggestion, not a tested recipe.

Sources and notes

  • Recipe: the dish type (roast, salad, pan-cooked greens or warm and fresh) is chosen from how each vegetable is usually cooked. Amounts are for 2 servings, in g as bought (the whole product, before trimming), with the edible part in g next to it. Times in min and temperatures in °C are typical values, so adjust to your oven and taste. This is generated cooking guidance, not data.
  • Nutrition: approximate amounts per 100 g of raw vegetable, USDA FoodData Central, provisional until Spain's BEDCA food database is imported. The card shows the share of the EU daily reference (Regulation 1169/2011, Annex XIII; fiber uses the EFSA 25 g) in one serving. Cooking changes nutrient amounts, so treat these as a guide. The Nutrient Density Score (0 to 100) is per 100 g of raw vegetable, not per calorie.
  • Prices: average Spanish consumer prices in € per kg as bought, July 2026, MAPA panel (Spain's Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food). Vegetables with a product-group price or an estimate are marked on the card.
  • PDF: the card is always drawn in the light theme so it prints well, and is a picture of the page (text can be copied from a hidden text layer). Illustrations come from the Vegetable Chart prototype. Page built with the Cool Graphs design system.
