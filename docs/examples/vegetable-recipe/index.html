Pick two. Get a recipe.

Choose any two of 27 vegetables and download a one-page A4 recipe card (PDF) for 2 servings, with amounts in g, times in min and oven temperatures in °C. The vegetables lead; extras such as chicken, eggs, yoghurt, rice or pasta are suggestions. Nutrition is per 100 g of raw vegetable (USDA, provisional) and prices are in € per kg (MAPA, July 2026). The method is a general suggestion, not a tested recipe.

Sources and notes

  • Recipe: the dish (tray bake, salad, soup, stir-fry, tortilla or frittata, pasta, curry, gratin, stuffed vegetables, fritters, pan-cooked greens or warm and fresh) is chosen from how the pair is usually cooked. Amounts are for 2 servings, in g as bought (the whole product, before trimming), with the edible part in g next to it. Times in min and temperatures in °C are typical values, so adjust to your oven and taste. Extras (meat, fish, eggs, dairy, rice, pasta, spices) are suggestions and are not counted in the nutrition or price numbers. This is generated cooking guidance, not data.
  • Nutrition: approximate amounts per 100 g of raw vegetable, USDA FoodData Central, provisional until Spain's BEDCA food database is imported. The card shows the share of the EU daily reference (Regulation 1169/2011, Annex XIII; fiber uses the EFSA 25 g) in one serving. Cooking changes nutrient amounts, so treat these as a guide. The Nutrient Density Score (0 to 100) is per 100 g of raw vegetable, not per calorie.
  • Prices: average Spanish consumer prices in € per kg as bought, July 2026, MAPA panel (Spain's Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food). Vegetables with a product-group price or an estimate are marked on the card.
  • PDF: the card is always drawn in the light theme so it prints well, and is a picture of the page (text can be copied from a hidden text layer). Illustrations come from the Vegetable Chart prototype. Page built with the Cool Graphs design system.
